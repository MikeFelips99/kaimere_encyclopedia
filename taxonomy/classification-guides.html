TAXONOMY · REFERENCE GUIDE

Taxonomic Ranks and Naming

This guide describes how taxonomic ranks, scientific names, synonyms, and provisional placements should be represented across the encyclopedia.

← Return to the Taxonomy Directory

Taxonomic ranks

Use ranks such as kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, and species when they are applicable and established. Some articles may instead describe clades or informal ecological groups; label those clearly.

Names and synonyms

Use the accepted name consistently throughout the site. Record synonyms, former combinations, common names, and alternate spellings where they are useful for searching, but distinguish them from the accepted name.

Uncertainty and proposed relationships

When a placement is uncertain, state what is known, what is proposed, and what evidence or setting decision would resolve the question. Do not silently convert a working hypothesis into a definitive classification.
