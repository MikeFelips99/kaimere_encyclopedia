Native Microbial Life

Kaimere’s native life is described in the setting as microscopic organisms commonly called “magic,” with intelligent and symbiotic characteristics.

← Return to the Taxonomy Directory

Overview

The native microbial biosphere is distinct from the waves of Earth life introduced through the Portal. The term “magic” is a common setting term; individual biological groups and mechanisms should be documented in dedicated articles as they are established.

Classification approach

Do not force native organisms into Earth taxonomic ranks without an explicit in-world or scientific rationale. Record their known traits, ecological roles, symbioses, and relationships separately from uncertain formal placement.
