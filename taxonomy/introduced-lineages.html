Portal-derived Organisms

This entry organizes Earth-derived organisms introduced to Kaimere through the Portal and links their taxonomic histories with the relevant evolutionary accounts.

← Return to the Taxonomy Directory

Overview

Organisms introduced through the Portal belong to many different Earth lineages. Their presence on Kaimere does not imply a single shared ancestry within the setting; they retain their respective evolutionary origins while entering new ecological communities.

How to document introductions

For each group, record its source lineage, introduction period where known, establishment history, subsequent local evolution, and any uncertainty about its current taxonomic placement.
