TAXONOMY ARTICLE GUIDANCE

Directory: pages/taxonomy.html
Reusable template: taxonomy/taxon_article_template.html

Suggested structure:
- Overview and scope
- Classification and rank
- Hierarchy (link to parent and child taxa)
- Diagnostic traits / definition
- Evolutionary history (link to Evolution)
- Known diversity (link to family and species pages)
- Distribution and ecology (link to Regions)
- Taxonomic notes, synonyms, and uncertainty
- Related articles

When adding an article, copy the template into the appropriate folder, update all relative paths for its depth, and add a card to pages/taxonomy.html. Do not treat navigation trees as a substitute for a sourced or canonically established phylogeny. Mark provisional placements clearly.
