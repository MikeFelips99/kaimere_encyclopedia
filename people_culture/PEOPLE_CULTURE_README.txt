PEOPLE & CULTURE ARTICLE GUIDANCE

Directory: pages/people_culture.html
Reusable template: people_culture/culture_article_template.html

Suggested entry types:
- Peoples and communities
- States, empires, confederations, and other polities
- Settlements and culturally important places
- Beliefs, rituals, customs, and social practices
- Trade, economy, diplomacy, and interregional contact

When adding an article, copy the template into a suitable folder, update relative paths, and add a card to pages/people_culture.html. Separate a community's own perspective from outsider descriptions and distinguish established worldbuilding from details that are still undecided.
