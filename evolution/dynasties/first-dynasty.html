Overview

An early stable period associated with a Late Devonian-like stage of introduced life and ecosystem development.

Historical position

The First Dynasty represents an early major interval in the history of introduced life on Kaimere. Its ecological context has been described as analogous in broad terms to Earth's Late Devonian.

Ecological development

As with other dynastic periods, its significance is tied to the organisms introduced during the interval and the ecosystems that developed after their arrival.

Chronology

The dynasty framework organizes Kaimere's natural history around the stability of Portal territory and the succession of introduced faunas.
