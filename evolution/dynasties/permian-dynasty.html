Overview

The longest of Kaimere's dynasties, marking an extended period in the history of introduced and diversifying organisms.

Historical position

The Permian Dynasty is identified as the longest of Kaimere's dynasties. Its length gives it an especially important place in the encyclopedia's broad chronology.

Faunal history

The period should be understood through the organisms introduced during its span, the lineages that persisted, and the ecological changes that accumulated over time.

Interpretation

The name provides a historical analogy rather than a guarantee that every feature of the interval matches Earth's Permian Period.
