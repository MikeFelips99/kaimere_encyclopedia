Historical position

The Tyrant Dynasty is associated with major evolutionary radiations in Kaimere's later natural history.

Megaraptoran radiations

During this broad interval, volcanic activity and ecological change are associated with two major radiations: Earth-derived Megaraptoridae and the Kaimere-evolved Agnostornia, including Styraconychidae and Apeirovenatoridae.

Evolutionary consequences

The divergence of introduced lineages and Kaimere-evolved clades is an important example of how introductions, isolation, and local ecological conditions interact in the setting.
