A succession of dynastic periods associated with Mesozoic lineages and major changes in Kaimere's faunas.

Historical position

The Mesozoic Dynasties comprise a succession of dynastic intervals associated with Mesozoic lineages and major faunal changes.

Evolutionary significance

These intervals provide the historical setting for the establishment and diversification of several reptilian and dinosaurian groups. Specific lineages should be linked to the dynasty in which they were introduced or diversified.

Chronology

The plural form is important: this is a sequence of dynasties rather than one uninterrupted period.
