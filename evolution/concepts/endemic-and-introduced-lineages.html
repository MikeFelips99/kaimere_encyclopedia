Overview

A guide to distinguishing organisms introduced through the Portal from lineages native to Kaimere or evolved there afterward.

Introduced organisms

Introduced lineages are organisms whose ancestors arrived through the Portal from Earth. Their later descendants may have evolved substantially after becoming established on Kaimere.

Endemic lineages

Endemic organisms are native to Kaimere or restricted to its biota, depending on how the term is being used in a particular article. Native microscopic “magic” is distinct from the Earth-derived organisms introduced through the Portal.

Why the distinction matters

Origin, present distribution, and evolutionary history are different questions. An Earth-derived lineage may become uniquely adapted to Kaimere, while a lineage that evolved on Kaimere may later spread across multiple regions.
