Overview

The history of introduced Megaraptoridae and Kaimere-evolved megaraptoran lineages, including Agnostornia.

Early history

Basal megaraptorans originated in Laurasia and descended from forms related to Fukuiraptor. The lineage first appeared in the broad interval of approximately 110–85 million years ago.

Two evolutionary histories

Kaimere contains both Earth-derived Megaraptoridae and a distinct Kaimere-evolved megaraptoran radiation. The latter includes Agnostornia, with the families Styraconychidae and Apeirovenatoridae.

Later radiations

During the Tyrant Dynasty, volcanic activity and ecological disruption are associated with major radiations in both the introduced Megaraptoridae and the endemic Agnostornia. Individual species accounts should link to this history where relevant.
