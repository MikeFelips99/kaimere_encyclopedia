Overview

A major mountain system that divides Arvel's humid north and east from its drier south and west.

Relief and structure

The Arvelith Highlands form a major mountain barrier across Arvel. Elevations commonly reach six to seven kilometres, making the range a dominant influence on the continent's physical geography.

Climate

Moist air arriving from the Trakaiam gyre rises over the highlands, producing orographic precipitation. The resulting rain shadow contributes to the drier conditions of southern and western Arvel, in contrast with the more humid north and east.

Regional significance

The range separates distinct climatic zones and helps structure the continent's biomes, river systems, and animal distributions.
