Overview

An isolated island in the Great Khalin Sea, home to the Tsuu Henjin and a distinctive maritime culture.

Setting

Blackfish Island lies within the Great Khalin Sea and is associated with the Tsuu Henjin, whose cultural and political life is deeply connected to the surrounding ocean.

Ecology and isolation

Island isolation helps make local marine resources and nearby apex predators central to the region's ecology. The surrounding waters form an essential part of the island's environmental setting.

People and place

The Tsuu Henjin are known as a culturally unified but politically decentralized maritime people. Their reputation among outsiders differs sharply from their own understanding of their identity and traditions.
