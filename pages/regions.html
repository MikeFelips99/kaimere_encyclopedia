A GUIDE TO REGIONS

A World Shaped by Geography

Kaimere's natural history is inseparable from its geography. Mountain systems redirect warm, moisture-bearing winds; shallow seas sustain immense marine ecosystems; and isolated islands develop distinctive biological and cultural histories.

This directory organizes regional articles from major landmasses to individual landscapes and island groups. Each article focuses on physical geography, geological history, climate, ecosystems, and connections to related regions and species.
