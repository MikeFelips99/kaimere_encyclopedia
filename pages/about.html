The World of Kaimere

A World Apart

Kaimere is a distant planet whose living world combines organisms originating from different periods of Earth's evolutionary history with a distinctive native biosphere. Its landscapes support a wide diversity of terrestrial, freshwater, and marine environments, shaped by geography, climate, ecological interactions, and successive biological communities.

The planet's natural history is inseparable from the Portal, the mechanism through which Earth life was introduced into Kaimere. Over time, introduced organisms established populations, diversified, and became components of ecosystems that differ substantially from those found on modern Earth.

Kaimere is also inhabited by intelligent peoples whose societies developed within this unusual biological world. Their histories, settlements, and relationships with the surrounding environment form another part of the planet's wider story.

Planetary Scale

Climate

A generally warmer, greenhouse-like climate with tropical, temperate, semi-arid, and arid environments.

Living World

Earth-derived organisms coexist with native microscopic life known as magic.

Ecological History

The Portal

Two Histories of Life Meet

The Portal has introduced organisms from Earth in successive waves, transforming ecosystems and creating a layered biological history. In Kaimere's chronology, the term Dynasty describes major intervals associated with Portal influence and relative territorial stability, providing a framework for discussing faunal change and extinction.

Kaimere also possesses native microscopic life commonly called magic. These endemic organisms are intelligent and symbiotic, and their interactions with other life are central to the setting's biology. They are distinct from organisms introduced through the Portal.

The Portal's jurisdiction and influence have changed over time. The region generally discussed as the Known World has shifted historically, so its boundaries should be understood in their particular historical context rather than as permanent planetary borders.

Using This Encyclopedia

How the Articles Fit Together

Begin with a directory when you want to browse a subject, or use links within an article to follow related topics. A regional entry may lead to its ecosystems and species; a taxonomic entry may lead to individual species and evolutionary accounts; and a cultural entry may connect a people or settlement to its geography and history.

Kaimere is an evolving worldbuilding project. Some articles document established setting information, while others may be expanded as details develop. Proposed classifications, uncertain relationships, and incomplete histories should be identified as such rather than presented as settled facts.
