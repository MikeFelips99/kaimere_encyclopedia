A GUIDE TO PEOPLE & CULTURE

The peoples of the Known World

Kaimere's human and Kaimeran societies are shaped by their environments, histories, political institutions, and relationships with neighbouring peoples. Cultural entries document communities on their own terms rather than reducing them to outside stereotypes.

This directory separates peoples and communities from the states that govern them, the settlements they inhabit, and the practices that may be shared across multiple cultures. Where an article is still being developed, it provides a structured starting point without assuming details that have not yet been established.
