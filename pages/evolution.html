A GUIDE TO EVOLUTION

A natural history unlike Earth's

Kaimere's evolutionary history combines native microscopic life, successive waves of organisms introduced through the Portal, and lineages that diversified under the planet's own ecological conditions.

The evolution directory connects broad historical periods with lineage-specific accounts and articles explaining evolutionary processes. Dynasty names organize major intervals in the setting's history; they are not intended to imply that every lineage evolved in isolation or that each interval maps perfectly onto Earth's geological timescale.

Interpreting Kaimere's evolutionary timeline

The Portal's introductions and the changing stability of its territory are central to the setting's chronology. Articles should distinguish Earth-derived organisms, endemic native life, and lineages that evolved on Kaimere after introduction.
