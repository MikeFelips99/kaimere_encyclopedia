A GUIDE TO TAXONOMY

Organizing the Diversity of Life

Taxonomy provides a structured way to describe organisms and communicate their relationships. On Kaimere, that structure must account for both endemic life and organisms introduced from Earth.

Entries distinguish formal taxonomic placement from broader ecological groupings and from evolutionary relationships that remain provisional. Follow a hierarchy downward to narrower groups, or use an article to move between taxa, species, regions, and evolutionary history.
